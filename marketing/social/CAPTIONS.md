### Posting notes
- **Stories (1080×1920):** keep stickers and the reply bar clear of the bottom ~15% and the top ~12%. The designs already leave the top band for the brand.
- **Alt text:** describe the screen, e.g. "StepPool app showing a live leaderboard with a 1,200-credit prize pool."
- **Before launch:** the images say "Free · iPhone & Android". If the Play Store listing isn't live yet, say "iPhone now, Android soon" in the caption.
- **Cash challenges are off.** Don't advertise paid entry or cash winnings until that's legally cleared and switched on.
